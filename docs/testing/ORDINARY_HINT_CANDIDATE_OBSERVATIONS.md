with three linked leaves. True Ascending/Twin aliases share one physical tree.

max255 entry digest; primary indexes, unrelated tables and all padding records

for this committed profile. Later counter-writing behavior requires a separately
bound consumer and expectation record.

The engineering serializer compresses full key-plus-ROW bytes. Public writer
prefix and scratch choices are observations, not engineering goldens. For example,

and 3,202 active bytes. Neither difference was repaired by adapting a golden after
a native result.

The proof reconstructs all selected framing and compares the entire original

129,612 logical index entries including aliases, 120 physical-tree instances and
34,603,008 original-prefix bytes. This includes opaque original DATA/LVAL gaps,
padding pages, unselected fields/indexes and map-container bytes outside the
qualified row/cell changes. Every appended page has exactly one typed purpose.

changes only repository-root/import composition from its frozen private source;
the [allocator model](ordinary_hint_growth_candidates/allocation_model.py) is

```sh

```

The [compact receipt](results/ace16-ordinary-hint-candidates-2026-10-27.json)
pins the exact reports, artifacts, inputs and maintained sources. The working

semantic no-ops and no observed index rebase. It does not qualify normal saves,
Microsoft Access execution, data macros, arbitrary page reuse/reclamation, general
map-window growth or complete ACE 16 compatibility. Existing broader literal-test
coverage and future compatibility work must retain their separate evidence.
