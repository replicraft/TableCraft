the initial failures, immutable successors and independent reader evidence.

The AllowZeroLength family crosses both General index versions with explicitly
stored Boolean true/false properties. Fourteen original/literal images cover

NoRequired action Source. Fourteen inert SDK reports agree. These source checks
do not yet establish the matching INSERT outcomes.

flags, LAST words, primary keys, rows and complete property bags. It preserves

without evaluating either. Two candidate Increment outcomes derive from original

SDK reports check their stored representation; no generator or normal generated
INSERT is called.
