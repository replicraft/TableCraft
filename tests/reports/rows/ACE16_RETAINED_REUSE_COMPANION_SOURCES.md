entry counts. Its capacity, complete key+ROW populations and full fences are
specified independently. This is a format-valid storage policy, not a claim to
reproduce another engine's UPDATE page partition byte for byte.

shared pages containing distinct slots. They qualify no new allocation or chunk
retirement. A static report projection initially confused the fixed `ID` column
with a request identifier; the frozen successor restricts that projection to
Blob/Memo. The model and raw byte laws remained unchanged before its first run.

deleted physical slot. New companion sources retain LAST 0/137 and their existing
root bytes. They are BEFORE-only evidence; generated outcomes require a separate
original-intent model and write proof. No source flag grants mutation permission.
